Ion-Current Playground

Every action potential is a tug-of-war between a dozen ionic currents. Pick one of the engines that cardiac_core ships, scale any current's conductance, and watch the AP change shape in real time — each trace is an exact single-cell simulation from the real engine, not a cartoon.

Four ionic engines are available. TTP06 (ten Tusscher–Panfilov 2006) and ORd (O'Hara–Rudy 2011) are human-ventricular models with endocardial, epicardial, and M-cell variants. PHAS13 (Paci 2013) is a human induced-pluripotent-stem-cell cardiomyocyte — it beats spontaneously, driven by the funny current If; turn gf down and its automaticity slows, to zero and it goes quiet. MHAS13 is a matured PHAS13 with the funny current switched off.

The three Combine sliders tune the headline morphology drivers together (sodium sets the upstroke, L-type calcium the plateau, rapid K⁺ the repolarization). The Isolate control lets you perturb a single current while everything else is held at baseline — the cleanest way to see what each one does, including the ones you may not recognize: the funny current, the Na/Ca exchanger, the Na/K and Ca pumps, and the background leaks (marked ★).

Try: on PHAS13, drag gf from 0 to 2× and watch the spontaneous rate climb. On TTP06 EPI, isolate I_Kr and cut it to 0.5× — the plateau stretches and APD₉₀ lengthens, the same mechanism behind drug-induced long-QT. Compare ENDO vs M-cell to see the intrinsic APD gradient across the ventricular wall.
