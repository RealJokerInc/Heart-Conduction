## Phase 1: Add archive system

**Goal**: `/blueprint` auto-generates an archive step in every PLAN.md, and `plans/` directories exist for all questions.
**Tier**: small

### Phase Context
The archive step goes in the Final Cleanup section of every generated PLAN.md. It copies the current PLAN.md to `plans/{date}_{slug}.md` before reverting the tmux pane. The `plans/` folder is created by `/research-new` for new questions and bootstrapped for existing ones.

### Step 1.1: Update `/blueprint` Final Cleanup template
**Model**: opus

#### Read First
- `.claude/skills/blueprint/SKILL.md:173-182` — current Final Cleanup template

#### Why
Every completed plan is a record of what was implemented and how. Without archiving, each `/blueprint` overwrites the previous plan. The archive step must be auto-generated so it's never forgotten.

#### Implementation Spec
**Files to modify:** `.claude/skills/blueprint/SKILL.md`

In the "Plan closing" section of `/blueprint` SKILL.md, find the line that says `**Always include in Final Cleanup**: Revert the bottom tmux pane` (around line 177). INSERT THE FOLLOWING TEXT immediately BEFORE that line:

    **Always include in Final Cleanup**: Archive the completed plan before clearing the slot:
    mkdir -p Research/Active/{question}/plans
    cp Research/Active/{question}/PLAN.md "Research/Active/{question}/plans/$(date +%Y-%m-%d)_{plan-slug}.md"
    Where {plan-slug} is derived from the PLAN.md title (lowercase, spaces/special chars to hyphens, max 60 chars).

This must appear BEFORE the tmux pane revert block so the plan is archived before the pane switches back to WHITEBOARD.

### Step 1.4: Fix `/blueprint` pane switch reliability
**Model**: opus

#### Read First
- `.claude/skills/blueprint/SKILL.md:194-205` — current Step 3b (separate pane switch step)
- `.claude/skills/blueprint-revise/SKILL.md:74-85` — current Step 6b (same pattern, needs same fix)

#### Why
The pane switch is currently Step 3b — a separate step after writing PLAN.md. In long conversations (blueprint is 210+ lines), this step gets forgotten because instructions fade from context. Two fixes: (1) move the switch instruction to be IMMEDIATELY inside Step 3 after the Write call (not a separate step), (2) also spawn a background agent to do the switch as a safety net.

#### Implementation Spec
**Files to modify:** `.claude/skills/blueprint/SKILL.md`

1. Remove Step 3b as a separate section
2. Add to the END of Step 3 (after "Write the plan to..."): "**Immediately after writing PLAN.md**, switch the bottom pane AND spawn a background agent as safety net:"
   ```markdown
   After writing PLAN.md, IMMEDIATELY do both:
   1. Switch bottom tmux pane to show PLAN.md (direct Bash call)
   2. Spawn a background Agent to verify the switch happened (safety net — if the direct call was skipped, the agent catches it)
   ```
3. Apply same pattern to `/blueprint-revise` Step 6b

#### Checklist
- [ ] Remove separate Step 3b from `/blueprint`
